# Module Two Team Project Development Plan

## 1. Game Scenario and Additional Elements
- Idea suggested by Troy Thomas -

Our team has chosen to develop a Call of Duty Zombies-inspired PvE (Player vs. Environment) survival game using Unreal Engine. Players must survive increasingly difficult waves of enemies, with each completed round introducing greater challenges and stronger enemy encounters.

The game will feature a branching progression system that gives players the option to continue surviving through additional rounds, activate an optional exfiltration sequence to secure a victory, or complete a map-specific Easter Egg quest to achieve an alternative objective.

The four additional elements selected for our game scenario are:

1. **Ammunition and Reloading System:** Players must manage their ammunition during combat by reloading their weapons when necessary. The system will include limited ammunition reserves and functional reload mechanics to encourage players to manage their resources until an options comes up to ressuply while surviving enemy waves.
2. **Weapon Progression and Upgrades:** Players will have access to multiple weapons and a weapon upgrade system that allows them to improve their combat effectiveness.
3. **Perks and Abilities:** Players can purchase perks or abilities through designated machines to improve their survivability and combat performance.
4. **Exfiltration and Easter Egg Objectives:** Players can choose to exfiltrate and secure a victory or pursue map-specific Easter Egg objectives for an alternative gameplay experience.

## 2. Game Content Brainstorming
- Ideas suggested by Daylen and Troy -
- 
Our game will focus on replayability, survival, progression, and player decision-making. The following features and content will help establish the core gameplay experience:

- **Round-Based Survival:** Players fight increasingly difficult waves of enemies as they progress through each round.
- **Enemy Variety:** Different enemy types will introduce unique combat challenges and require players to adjust their strategies.
- **Weapon Selection:** Players will have access to a variety of weapons suited for different combat situations.
- **Weapon Upgrade Machine:** A machine or device that allows players to upgrade their weapons to improve damage and effectiveness.
- **Perk/Ability Machine:** Players can purchase perks or abilities that provide advantages such as improved health, movement, or combat performance.
- **Map Exploration:** Players can explore different areas of each map and discover useful resources, machines, and objectives.
- **Exfiltration System:** Players can choose to activate an optional exfiltration event to escape the map and secure a victory.
- **Easter Egg Objectives:** Each map will have its own optional Easter Egg quest that players can complete to achieve an alternative objective or ending.
- **Increasing Difficulty:** Enemy health, damage, quantity, or other difficulty factors will increase as players advance through rounds.
- **Pause Functionality:** Players will be able to pause gameplay when necessary.

These features will be developed in stages, with priority given to establishing a playable survival experience before implementing additional mechanics and polishing existing systems.

## 4. Alpha Stage Development Goals
- Ideas suggested by Daylen and Troy -
  
The Alpha submission will focus on establishing the foundation of our game and delivering a functional, playable survival experience. Our primary objective is to implement the essential gameplay elements needed to demonstrate the overall direction and core mechanics of the project.

The targeted Alpha features include:

- **At least one playable map:** Establish at least one playable environment that demonstrates the game's map design and survival gameplay.
- **At Least Five Different Weapons:** Implement a minimum of five usable weapons that players can equip and use against enemies.
- **At Least Two Enemy Types:** Introduce at least two distinct enemy types to provide variety in combat encounters.
- **Perk/Ability Machine:** Implement at least one functional perk or ability machine that allows players to purchase an in-game advantage.
- **Pause Functionality:** Implement a working pause system that allows players to temporarily stop gameplay.
- **Optional Exfiltration:** Implement a functional exfiltration system that gives players the option to escape and secure a victory rather than continuing through additional rounds.

**Deferred Alpha Features:**

The following systems are part of our planned game design but may be postponed until the Beta stage to prioritize the core gameplay experience:

- **Ramping Round Difficulty:** Implement progressively increasing enemy difficulty as players advance through rounds.
- **Weapon Upgrade Machine:** Implement a functional weapon upgrade system that allows players to improve their weapons.

**Alpha Progress Evaluation:**

Progress will be evaluated based on whether the required features have been implemented and can be demonstrated in a playable build. Features should be functional enough to showcase their intended purpose, even if they have placeholder assets, basic animations, or limited visual polish.

The team will track completed features, identify incomplete mechanics, and document any issues that need to be addressed before moving into Beta development.

## 5. Beta Stage Development Goals
- Ideas suggested by Daylen and Troy -

After completing the Alpha objectives, the team will focus on expanding the game's functionality, implementing deferred systems, improving gameplay balance, and preparing the project for its final submission.

The targeted Beta features include:

- **Complete Round Progression System:** Implement a functional round-based system that increases enemy difficulty as players progress through each round.
- **Weapon Upgrade Machine:** Implement the weapon upgrade machine, allowing players to purchase upgrades and improve weapon performance.
- **Expanded Enemy Functionality:** Improve enemy behavior, combat mechanics, animations, and difficulty balancing.
- **Perk/Ability Improvements:** Expand the existing perk system by improving functionality and introducing additional player advantages where feasible.
- **Exfiltration Improvements:** Refine the exfiltration sequence, enemy encounters, and victory conditions to provide a complete gameplay experience.
- **Easter Egg Objectives:** Develop and implement map-specific Easter Egg quests with functional objectives and completion conditions.
- **Map Improvements:** Refine both playable maps, improve environmental details, and address any level design issues.
- **Weapon Balancing:** Adjust weapon damage, effectiveness, and usability to maintain balanced gameplay.
- **Gameplay Polish:** Improve user interface elements, sound effects, animations, visual effects, and overall player feedback.
- **Bug Fixing and Optimization:** Identify and resolve gameplay bugs, improve performance, and address issues discovered during testing.

**Beta Progress Evaluation:**
- Ideas suggested by Daylen and Troy -
  
Progress will be evaluated based on the functionality, stability, and completeness of the implemented systems. The team will test gameplay mechanics, verify that systems work together correctly, and identify any remaining bugs or balancing issues.

By the end of Beta, the game should represent a substantially complete experience with its primary mechanics implemented and working together. Remaining work should primarily involve refinement, optimization, testing, and final corrections.

## 8. Task Assignment and Progress Reporting

Task assignments and development updates will be coordinated through Discord direct messages and GitHub branching.

Each team member will communicate directly with the collaborator responsible for the relevant development role when assigning tasks, requesting assistance, or reporting progress.

GitHub branching will be used to organize individual development contributions and track changes made to the project.

When making updates through GitHub, team members should:

- Create or work within an appropriate branch for their assigned task.
- Clearly explain what changes were made to the project.
- Identify which features, assets, or systems were modified or added.
- Document any problems or unfinished work.
- Explain what needs to be completed next.

This approach will allow team members to review each other's contributions, understand the current state of development, and coordinate future tasks while minimizing conflicts when merging changes into the main project branch.
